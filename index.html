Welcome to
beautiful Tripura

Whether you are coming from nearby or from across the country, we look forward to welcoming you to the place where we will celebrate together.

Arriving by air

The nearest major airport for many guests is Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (IXA) in Agartala. Please check current flight availability for your travel dates.

Travelling by train

Agartala (AGTL) and Kumarghat (KUGT) have railway stations. Check IRCTC for routes and timings before booking.

Between the celebrations

The wedding takes place in Kumarghat on 13 December, and the reception in Agartala on 17 December. Plan a separate transfer between the two towns. Local taxis and buses are available; arrange intercity transport ahead of time and confirm fares and journey times with your provider.

What should I wear?

Festive traditional attire will be lovely for the wedding; festive or formal attire will be appropriate for the reception. Comfortable shoes and a light shawl or jacket are a good idea for cooler evenings. These are suggestions — please wear what makes you feel comfortable.

A little more of Tripura

If you are extending your stay, explore the rock carvings at Unakoti, Ujjayanta Palace in Agartala or Neermahal on Rudrasagar Lake. Check opening times and travel arrangements with Tripura Tourism before setting out.

Local flavours & hospitality

Leave a little room to discover Tripura’s local tea, pineapples and regional cuisine. Ask your hosts about their favourite places, and let them know about any dietary needs before your visit.
