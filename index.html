Exploring Tripura

Essential travel details to help you comfortably navigate, explore, and experience Tripura during your visit.

Reaching Tripura & Traveling Between Venues

By Air (MBB Airport - Agartala)

Fly into Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (IXA) in Agartala. Direct and connecting flights operate daily from Kolkata, Guwahati, Delhi, and Bengaluru.

By Train

Kumarghat (KUGT) and Agartala (AGTL) are directly connected by broad-gauge express trains.

Transit between Kumarghat (13th) & Agartala (17th):

Kumarghat is approximately 120 km from Agartala. The journey takes around 2.5 to 3 hours via highway taxi or express train, offering scenic views of tea estates and green rolling hills.

Sightseeing Highlights in Tripura

Near Kumarghat

Unakoti Rock Reliefs

Ancient pilgrimage site featuring magnificent 8th-9th century rock-cut carvings of Shiva nestled in lush greenery.

~30 mins from Kumarghat
In Agartala

Ujjayanta Palace

A majestic royal palace built in 1901 by the Manikya dynasty, set among tiled fountains and neoclassical gardens.

City Center, Agartala
Near Agartala

Neermahal Water Palace

The picturesque "Water Palace" of North East India sitting in the middle of Rudrasagar Lake.

~1 hr drive from Agartala

Weather & Clothing Advice

The weather is pleasant with clear skies. Evenings can be cool, so carrying a light jacket or shawl for outdoor evening celebrations is recommended.
